**One concrete P1 found and corrected: initial data and the oracle were transposed.** The former `initial_line[:, :, None]` varied along the second-to-last array dimension. Native state arrays are `(component, *reversed(cells))`, explicitly required by `python/pops/runtime/_bind_validation.py:161`: in Dim=2 this is `(component, y, x)`. Thus the purported x shock tube was a y discontinuity with periodic rather than Outflow boundaries, while momentum was still initialized for x. The former invariance comparison also tested constancy in x. The author corrected both initial and exact arrays to `line[:, None, :]` and the transverse invariance test to `actual - actual[:, :1, :]`. Current source was reread after this correction. This defect was established by the array contract and source inspection, not a native run.

The physical conventions are consistent. The state stores total energy **density** E, with internal energy density `E - |m|²/(2ρ)`. The stiffened EOS is `p=(γ−1)(E−|m|²/(2ρ))−γp∞`, hence the initial internal density is `(p+γp∞)/(γ−1)`. The oracle and example use this convention; neither confuses specific internal energy e with E. Flux energy is `(E+p)v`; sound speed is `sqrt(γ(p+p∞)/ρ)`. Under the pressure shift q=p+p∞, the corresponding ideal-gas energy is E'=E−p∞. This constant energy-density offset is explicitly checked in the new tests.

The mass/energy-constant oracle and momentum increment `t*(p_L-p_R)` are mathematically correct for the stationary far fields on this unit-area domain while the physical waves remain inside. The revised `(1.4,.3)` stiffened case meets that condition. **Physical wave containment does not prove that a first-order numerical solution has negligible tails at an Outflow boundary.** If the strict native balances fail, inspect actual numerical boundary fluxes before attributing the discrepancy to nonconservation; do not silently relax the predeclared thresholds.

Limits: this review did not execute a native M03 run, MPI, GPU, or a convergence campaign. The density thresholds, strict decrease, saved-state conservation, and transverse invariance are acceptance criteria still requiring authenticated runtime receipts. The 100 pressure-bisection iterations reach the checked residual tolerance for the chosen finite positive-pressure family; they are not a general scale-independent root-error bound. The oracle intentionally rejects p_star<=0, including some otherwise admissible stiffened-gas states with p+p∞>0, and its bracket has an absolute 1e12 ceiling. Gauss integration of smooth fan pieces is numerical quadrature, not an exact-arithmetic closed-form integral; order comparisons and independent conservation checks bound its error for the tested cases. These limitations do not invalidate the specified positive-pressure M03 cases and are not claimed as general EOS qualification.
