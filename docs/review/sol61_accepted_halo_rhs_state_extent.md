and the exact resolved input binding. Version zero means unknown, including legacy

under their existing contracts; an unknown effect is rejected only when that
producer belongs to the requested strong accepted-Halo dependency closure.

and their transitive primitive definitions. It does not accept an extension's

zero. Its explicit traits pass through the actual AMR loader and attachment into
the retained RHS row; the row's exact Field contract includes the effect when
present. There is no model/species/formula dispatch.

The strong preflight, inside the existing voted request-preparation phase and
before the bulk Field materialization, backup allocation or Q staging, closes and orders
only the Field producers required by the retained typed BC dependencies. It
rejects unknown effects, positive extent (Q halo → Field → Q grown cycle),
uncovered InputAux bindings and required Field-BC cycles. A coupled method or a
separately authenticated causal InputAux effect is required for those routes;
no repeated solve or assumed cell-local callback substitutes for that contract.

For the admitted direct valid-cell RHS route the temporary solve uses the complete
staged candidate hierarchy, its actual authenticated level point and the existing
typed Field boundary route. The helper invokes no auxiliary refresh and no global
Field DAG ordering. Genuine solved FieldOutputs are copied temporarily for BC
reads. Unrelated dirty dependent Aux are not executed: their prior images and
provenance are restored with the Q/Field backups before publication. This avoids
claiming physical time/dt/fraction semantics from `AuxiliaryEvaluationPoint`, which
does not carry those values, and avoids exposing the new route to legacy refresh
allocations before votes.
