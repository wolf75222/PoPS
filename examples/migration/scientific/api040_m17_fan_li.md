# M17/W08 Fan–Li15 reception (prepared 2026-09-29)

This case transcribes the pinned `PoPS_Codex_handoff_0.4.0/reference/PoPS_API_v0.4.0/examples/moments.py` Gaussian mixture, q-outer 15-moment ordering, and four-point Gauss straight raw-moment path. The historical `tests/check_more_cases.py` check used four 1D cells, `dt=1e-4`, `t=4e-4`; corpus M17 instead requests `N=16` and eight SSPRK2 steps. This script sets `dt=1e-4`, `t=8e-4` on a **16×16 Dim=2** periodic grid with x-dependent states and y extrusion. It makes no Dim=1 or AMR qualification claim.

The equation is `U_t + div F(U) + B_x(U) U_x + B_y(U) U_y = 0`. Both conservative flux and the complete physical nonconservative matrices come from `pops.moments.fan_li.fan_li15_expressions`. The sole numerical transport method is `PathConservativeFiniteVolume` with public `SymbolicPath`, Gauss4 quadrature, first-order reconstruction and Rusanov flux. The declared speed is the library's raw-second-moment majorant `sqrt(6+sqrt(10))*sqrt(M20/rho)` (and `M02` in y), maximized at endpoints. For a straight raw path with positive density, the interior second-moment/density ratio is a convex combination of endpoint ratios. This is a conditional bound on the **complete** `DF+B`, not a realizability theorem. Every explicit SSPRK2 state is checked by a Program reduction for `rho>0`, `theta_xx>0`, `theta_yy>0`, and `det(Theta)>0`; an invalid state fails rather than being clipped.

The adjacent independent oracle reproduces `tests/python/support/fan_li15_oracle.py`'s bivariate generating-function and Gaussian integration-by-parts construction; it does not call production `fan_li15_expressions`. It integrates the 1D semidiscrete periodic FV ODE by SciPy DOP853 with 24-point Gauss path quadrature, and checks selected paths again at 48 points, including density ratios 0.2 and 5. The predeclared full-run criteria are maximum error `3e-8`, ten-line inventory defect `3e-13`, Gauss24–48 path discrepancy `3e-8`, **Gauss4–48 path discrepancy at saved initial/final faces** `3e-8`, active nonconservative contribution `>1e-8`, transverse invariance `1e-12`, and canonical/reverse permutation error `1e-12`. The permutation threshold needs both saved canonical and reverse receipts; each individual run records its order and saved state.

The density-ratio stress calculation deliberately distinguishes the authored four-point rule from the 48-point reference. Its left endpoint is the exact initial N16 cell average at x-cell 0; its right endpoint is the adjacent x-cell 1 initial average multiplied in every raw component by the density ratio. For this precise pair, before any native M17 run, Gauss4–48 discrepancies were `7.33e-7` at ratio 0.2 and `3.70e-6` at ratio 5 (Gauss24–48 near `5e-17`). A separate pure unit test uses fixed mixtures with weights 0.55 and 0.58 and likewise finds a nonzero Gauss4 error; its numerical values are not the preceding N16-cell results. The four-point rule is not claimed accurate to `3e-8` over that wider contrast range. For the actual N16 initial interfaces, Gauss4–48 is `6.07e-18`; the saved-final check and full-run solution criterion will catch drift during the prescribed small-amplitude case. The contrast stress is a diagnostic of a known quadrature limit, not a second native full-case qualification.
